# Phase 1: Region & Scope Definition

## Chosen MVP Region & Election Type
**Geography/Region:** India (National/General)
**Election Type:** Lok Sabha (General Elections)

*Reasoning:* The project name `MatdaanPath` indicates an Indian context ("Voting Path"). The MVP will target the most common, wide-reaching election process in India to provide maximum initial value.

## In-Scope for MVP
- Guided election timeline for the Indian General Elections.
- Eligibility checker (Age, Citizenship, Voter ID Registration Status).
- Registration process and deadlines overview.
- General FAQ and Glossary (e.g., EVM, VVPAT, Constituency, ECI).
- Source-backed responses using official Election Commission of India (ECI) data.

## Out of Scope for MVP
- State Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) or Local Body (Panchayat/Municipal) elections.
- Live election result tracking.
- Personalized candidate discovery (showing specific candidates for a user's constituency).
- Full multilingual support (MVP will launch in English/Hindi basics, full localization in v2).
- Native iOS/Android apps (Web MVP first).
